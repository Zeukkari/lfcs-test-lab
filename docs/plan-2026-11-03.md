## 4. House style (contract for every new note)

Derived from lvm.md, firewalls-and-nat.md, acls.md, ip-ss-mtr.md:

- H1: `# <Domain> — <Topic>`; first paragraph:
  `Domain: <Domain> ("<exact competency wording>").`
- Dense and exam-task-shaped: command blocks, tables for per-family
  deltas (deb/rpm/suse columns), no tutorial prose, no intro/outro
  fluff.
- Explicit **trap** callouts (e.g. the `mask::` trap, fstab-reboot trap).
- Ends with (or embeds per-section) **grader-style verification**: fresh
  command + reboot-survivability (research implication 5 lists the
  canonical verifiers per topic).
- SUSE columns present but marked low exam ROI where relevant.
- Carry `[UNCERTAIN]` markers verbatim where the research flags them.
- Cross-link related notes and learning-log sections instead of
  repeating content.
- Target 60–200 lines per note; mdadm/LUKS/NBD-section stay lean.

## 8. Risks and edge cases

- **Duplication drift** between overlapping notes (p→21/18, u/w→29/22,
  SELinux specifics) — mitigated by the boundaries in §3; batch gate
  checks cross-links exist instead of repeated content.
- **Research gaps** (partitioning commands, systemd unit anatomy,
  routing persistence): standard stable knowledge, but each such section
  gets lab verification before its checklist box can ever be ticked;
  notes must not cite the research doc for them.
- **Note bloat**: Q4 classics and boot-recovery can balloon; hard cap
  ~200 lines, split or cut rather than exceed.
- **`[UNCERTAIN]` erosion**: markers must survive into notes
  (constraints scope, exam distro, AppArmor, libvirt grading, netplan
  point version); final gate greps for them.
- **Checklist semantics**: boxes stay **unchecked** — they mean "can do
  cold in exam time", not "note exists". This plan only closes the
  note gaps.
- Stale-fact risk: prices/task counts are point-in-time; notes should
  not duplicate them (they live in README/strategy only).
